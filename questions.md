# questions.md

1. What is my savings rate this month, and is it trending up or down
   over the trailing 6 months? (income − spend) / income, plotted
   month over month.

2. Which 3 spending categories have drifted furthest from my intended
   budget over the last 90 days, in absolute dollars and in percent?

3. How does my spending split across categories compare to the BLS
   Consumer Expenditure Survey benchmark for my age and income
   bracket — which categories am I above the benchmark share in, and
   by how many percentage points?

4. What share of my spending is fixed (rent, utilities, insurance,
   subscriptions) versus discretionary, and how does that ratio
   compare to the benchmark household?

5. Which transactions this month fall outside my own historical
   pattern (by category and amount), and by how many standard
   deviations / what anomaly score?

6. What is the forecasted spend by category for next month, and what
   is the stated error range on that forecast?

7. Which categories have the highest month-over-month volatility, and
   is that volatility rising or falling over the last 6 months?
